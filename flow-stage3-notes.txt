Stage 3 checkpoint — Home + Search inline video previews
Adds a single muted, 480p-capped Media3 preview player shared by Home and standard Search results. A regular video starts after 700 ms of scroll-idle dwell when sufficiently visible; scroll or lifecycle pause stops it and restores the thumbnail. List cards expand into the inline player layout with a progress line, muted/captions indicators, and title/channel details. Shorts are excluded, including the Shorts-only Search grid. Stages 1 and 2 remain as supplied because their landscape More videos drawer and related-feed chips/paging were already implemented.
